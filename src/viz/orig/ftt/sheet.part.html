Number options

Largest number15 of 30Gaps (1 m, 1.5 m), 13 points, 24 points, 12-month suspension, 4th gear for braking effort.
Smallest number10 of 301 year (probation, re-apply), 12 months, 2× wet, 1.6 mm, 7 pm, 1st/2nd gear for power.
Middle number5 of 30Only: 3 s signal (twice), 16 km/h, 1.5 m, 24 in 24 months.

Rule of thumb: pick the calm option that keeps a safe gap (slow down, stay back, move to the side, be patient). Not horn, not speed up, not stop dead, not overtake.

Numbers that look alike

Gaps

1 mPassing a parked vehicle
1 mPedestrians facing you (road with no pavement)
1.5 mPedestrians with their back to you

Cyclists: as much room as you'd give a car, because they may swerve.

Following and stopping

2–3Presses at short intervals for intermittent (on/off) braking

Always longer: behind a bus or lorry, on a wet road, with worn tread, with a loaded vehicle.

Times

7 pm–7 amHeadlights on by law
5–10 sCheck your mirrors this often, and before every lane change, overtake and turn
7:30–9:30Bus lane, weekday mornings (am)
5–8Bus lane, weekday evenings (pm)

Outside bus-lane hours you can use it. During them, never, even when other lanes are full.

Gears

1stMost torque. U-turns (1st or 2nd). Through a flood, slowly.
2ndMore power than 3rd or 4th; less braking effort to stop; stronger engine brake than 3rd
4thNeeds the most braking effort to stop
4th / 5th80 km/h on a clear road
4th → 3rdChange down before a bend (3rd or 2nd before a left turn)

Lower gear = more power, less speed, stronger engine brake. Change down before the bend or turn, never in it.
