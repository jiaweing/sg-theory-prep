Answer patterns

Counted across all 148 official questions. When you're unsure, these tip the odds.

Usually the right answer

"Stop if necessary" / "be ready to stop" / "be prepared to stop"Always
"Dip" / "dipped headlights"AlwaysHigh beam only on a poorly lit road with nothing ahead.
"Look over your shoulder"AlwaysBlind spots are what the mirrors can't show.
"Lower gear" / "1st gear"AlwaysEvery time it's about a flood.
"Charged in court" / "public transport" / "both"Always
"Move to the side" / "pull over" / "keep left" / "left lane"13 of 15Wrong as "increase your speed and move to the side", and "left only" on a one-way street (it's left or right).
"Slow down" / "reduce speed" / "drive slower"26 of 29Wrong as "slow down quickly" for a siren, or when you "signal the driver to slow down".
"Reduces" / "impaired" / "affects"13 of 14Alcohol and interior lights only ever make things worse. Seat belts reduce injury, not the chance of a collision.

Usually a trap

"Same speed" / "normal speed" / "maintain your speed" / "carry on" / "ignore"Never0 of 21.
"Improves" / "better" / "faster reaction" / "more alert"Never0 of 15.
"High beam" / "higher gear"Never0 of 9.
"Immediately"NeverSiren behind you: pull over, don't stop dead.
"Signal the driver" / "signal him"Never
"Speed up" / "faster" / "quickly" / "accelerate" / "increase speed"1 of 43Right only once you've already moved out to overtake: accelerate and finish quickly.
"Horn"1 of 12Right only to warn a car drifting in its lane while you overtake it.
"Only"1 of 16Right only for flashing amber: proceed only when it's safe. "Driver only", "fine only", "certain speed only" are all wrong.
"Mirror"2 of 13Right for moving off (mirrors, blind spot, then signal) and for ending an overtake (rear-view mirror). Never for a blind spot.

Rule of thumb: slow down, move aside, be ready to stop. Not horn, not speed up, not "keep your speed", not stop dead.
