Numbers that look alike

Gaps

1 mPassing a parked vehicle
1 mPedestrians facing you (road with no pavement)
1.5 mPedestrians with their back to you
1.5 mPassing a moving vehicle

Parked or can see you: 1 m. Moving or can't see you: 1.5 m. Cyclists: as wide as possible, as much room as a car, because they may swerve.

Free play and slack

15–20 mmFront brake lever
10–20 mmClutch lever
≤ 20 mmDrive chain slack. A loose chain can lock the rear wheel.

Everything tops out at 20. Brakes start bigger (15), the clutch starts at 10.

Bus lanes

7:30–9:30Monday to Saturday, morning
11:30–2:00Saturday, late morning to afternoon

Outside those hours you can use it. During them, only to avoid a collision, never because other lanes are full.

Gears

1stMost power
2ndStops the bike most effectively; more power than 3rd or 4th
4thNeeds the most braking effort to stop

Lower gear = more power, less speed, stronger engine braking. Change down to 3rd or 2nd before a slope, and use a low gear downhill, in floods, on uneven or winding roads.
